Trip Highlights

My recent getaway to Fire Island was an absolute breath of fresh air. From walking along the tranquil, untouched beaches and historic boardwalks to catching breathtaking sunset views near the iconic Fire Island Lighthouse, every moment felt like a peaceful escape from the bustling city. The pristine natural habitats, serene maritime forests, and ocean breeze made this coastal adventure unforgettable.
